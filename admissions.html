Eligibility

  • Intermediate (HSC) with Pre-Engineering, Pre-Medical or Computer Science, or equivalent.
  • Minimum marks as announced in the admission notice.
  • Pass the department entry test.

How to Apply

  1. Fill in the online application form.
  2. Upload your transcripts, CNIC copy and photograph.
  3. Pay the application fee.
  4. Appear in the entry test.
  5. Check the merit list and complete enrollment.
