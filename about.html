About the Department

The Department of Computer Science offers undergraduate and graduate education in computing. Our graduates work in software companies, government, research labs and startups.

Vision and Mission

Vision

To be a leading centre of computing education and research that produces skilled and ethical professionals.

Mission

To deliver quality teaching, encourage research and innovation, and build links with industry and the community.

Our History

  1. Establishment: The department started with a small group of students and faculty.
  2. Growth: New labs, programs and research groups were added.
  3. Today: A modern department with updated labs and an active student community.
